# Payments

**Dashboard → Payments** lists your LACORE purchases from the store: **date, package, amount** and
**status** (purchase, subscription, renewal, refund, chargeback, declined), with totals for
purchases and refunds at the top.

<Callout type="info">
  This is a purchase **history**, not a billing portal — there are no downloadable invoices or
  receipts here. For a formal receipt, use the confirmation Tebex emailed you at checkout.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
  **How purchases are matched.** Payments are linked to your account by the **Discord ID you enter at
  checkout**. If a purchase is missing, make sure you provided your Discord ID on the Tebex checkout,
  then open a ticket and we'll link it.
</Callout>

Delivery of the resource itself continues through **Tebex & Keymaster**, tied to your purchase — see
[Downloads & versions](/customer-portal/downloads) for the current version.

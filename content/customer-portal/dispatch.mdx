# Dispatch — live ops

**Dashboard → Dispatch** is a live CAD in the browser: your units on one side, your calls on the
other, and actions that reach the game.

<Callout type="warning">
  **This page drives a [LACORE for Roblox](/products/lacore-roblox) server.** For dispatching a
  **FiveM** server from a browser, use the [web dispatcher](/features/webdispatch), which runs on your
  own infrastructure and is gated by Discord role. The two are separate surfaces — do not expect this
  page to show your FiveM units.
</Callout>

## What you see

The unit table carries **unit**, **department**, **status**, **incident number** and **location**, with
a status control per row. Select a unit, then assign it to a call.

If nothing is connected, the page says **No live server** rather than showing an empty grid — if you
see that with a server running, the link between the game and your account is what to check first.

## What you can do

- Set a unit's status.
- Select a unit and **assign** it to a call.

Actions taken here reach the game, so treat it as a real dispatch seat rather than a monitor.

# Community page

Every licensed server gets a **public page on the LACORE portal**: the server's name, its
departments, and who is on duty right now — a roleplay website with a law-enforcement focus that
you do not have to build or host.

![The community page — push, portal, page](/img/changelog/community-page.svg)

## Getting one

Nothing to configure beyond what a licensed server already has:

1. `setr lacore_license_key "…"` in `server.cfg` (the key from **License keys**).
2. `Features.community = true` (the default).

Within two minutes of starting, the server pushes its first roster and the page exists at
`https://lacore.netica.dev/community/<id>`. The `<id>` is your licence key's record id — stable,
not guessable, and never the key itself. The server console prints the full URL once, on the
first accepted push:

```
[lacore] Community page live: https://lacore.netica.dev/community/k8f3…
```

## What the page shows

- **Header** — community name, online / last seen, players, on-duty count, a **Join the Discord**
  button (from `discordInvite` in `cfg-presence-sh.lua`) and your website.
- **Departments** — one card per agency from `cfg-agencies-sh.lua`, law enforcement first, with the
  number of units on duty. Fire / EMS and other services below.
- **On duty right now** — callsign, name, department, status. Refreshes every 30 seconds.
- **My unit** — an officer signed in to the portal with Discord sees their own row highlighted.

Anyone with the link may look; no sign-in is needed.

## Making it yours — Dashboard → Community

Departments and the roster come from the server. Everything else you write on **Dashboard →
Community**, one page per licence key:

- **Header** — tagline, accent colour (the page's brand colour), Discord invite and website. What
  you set here wins over the values in the server config.
- **About** — free text, and **Rules**, one per line, rendered as a numbered list.
- **Departments** — every department the server reports gets a row: an *About* blurb (shown on the
  card and the department page), *Requirements*, and a **Recruiting** switch.
- **Applications** — one switch to accept applications on the page, plus optional extra questions,
  one per line. Discord name, in-game name and a free text are always asked.

**SAVE PAGE** publishes immediately; the public page picks it up on its next refresh.

## Department pages

Every department card opens its own view at `/community/<id>/<DEPT>` — description, requirements,
the recruiting badge and who from that department is on duty. The tab row at the top of the page
switches between the overview and the departments, and a department link can be shared directly.

## Applications

With applications on and at least one department recruiting, the page shows **Apply** (top) and
**Apply to …** on each recruiting department. The form asks for Discord name, in-game name, your
extra questions and a free text; it is sessionless (players do not need a LACORE account), rate
limited per connection and protected by a honeypot. One open application per person and department.

They land under **Dashboard → Community → Applications**, newest first: **ACCEPT**, **DECLINE**
(with an optional note for your records) or **DELETE**. The applicant is not messaged by the portal —
you reach them on Discord with the name they gave. Up to 200 applications are kept per page.

### Forwarded to Discord

Under **Notifications** on the same page:

- **DM me on Discord** (default on) — the LACORE bot sends the account owner a direct message with
  the application: department, Discord and in-game name, the answers, the free text, and a link to
  the inbox. Needs DMs from server members allowed on the LACORE Discord.
- **Discord webhook** — paste a webhook URL from a channel in *your own* Discord (Channel settings →
  Integrations → Webhooks) and every application is posted there as an embed, for the whole
  recruiting team to see. Only `discord.com/api/webhooks/…` URLs are accepted; the URL is never shown
  on the public page.

Both fire when the application is stored; a failed notification never loses the application.

### The deep tier — your Discord, your roles, per department

Invite the LACORE bot into **your own** Discord and the notifications become yours to route:

1. **Discord connection** — *INVITE THE BOT* (it needs to see channels, send messages, embed links),
   paste your **server id**, *CHECK*. The dashboard then knows your roles and channels.
2. **Default targets** — a **channel** the bot posts every application into, **roles to ping** in
   that post, and **roles whose members get a DM**. The webhook stays available as well.
3. **Per department** — open a department row and switch on *Own notification targets*: the same
   pickers, for that department only. Anything not overridden inherits the defaults.

So LAPD applications can land in `#lapd-recruiting` with a ping for `@LAPD Command` while the
sheriff's go to their own channel, and the recruiter role gets a DM for every department.

Role DMs need the bot's members intent (`ENABLE_GUILD_MEMBERS=true` on the LACORE bot); the
connection check says whether it is on. Channel posts and pings work regardless. The free text of an
application can never ping anyone — only the roles you picked are allowed to.

## Many departments

A server with twenty agencies does not get twenty tabs. Above seven departments the tab row becomes
a **department picker**, cards are ordered **recruiting first, then by units on duty**, the grid
turns compact, and everything past the first eight folds behind **Show all**. In the dashboard the
department list is an accordion with a filter box and a **Recruiting only** switch; each row's
header shows RECRUITING / DESCRIBED / EMPTY without opening it.

## What leaves your server

Branding, the agency list (short + long name + type), and per on-duty unit: callsign, nick,
department, job, status. The Discord id travels with the roster **only** so the portal can match a
signed-in officer to their row — it is stripped from everything the page or the API returns.

Nothing is stored: the roster lives in memory on the portal and is gone when the server stops
pushing (the page then says *last seen a while ago*).

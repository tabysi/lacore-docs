# Surveys & rebate

LACORE runs two questionnaires from the customer portal. The **community survey** is one *you* start
and share with your players; the **customer survey** asks licence holders about price, fit and
whether they are staying. For every **verified** answer your community collects, you earn a rebate on
your next purchase — up to **50 %**.

<Callout type="info">
  **Dashboard → Surveys.** You need a licence key with at least one server reporting activity, because
  that is what the quota is measured against.
</Callout>

## How a campaign works

<Steps>

### You start a campaign

Pick the wave, confirm the participation terms, and the portal freezes your **denominator** — the
number of active accounts your servers have reported. Frozen means frozen: deleting player records
afterwards changes nothing.

### You share the link

Every campaign has its own `/s/…` link. Participants sign in with Discord — not with a LACORE
account — answer, and can withdraw their answer afterwards from the link on the closing page.

### Answers are verified

Each answer is classified, and only *verified* ones count toward your rebate.

### The rebate is booked

`verified ÷ frozen denominator` gives your quota, capped at 50 %, applied to your next purchase and
recorded in an append-only ledger. Corrections are counter-entries, never edits.

</Steps>

## What decides whether an answer counts

Your own player data is treated as a **signal**, not as proof — it reaches LACORE from your server,
uploaded by the person the rebate pays. What actually decides is Discord:

| Check | Why |
|---|---|
| Discord account age | Read from the account's snowflake — fresh throwaway accounts do not count |
| Membership of your guild | The answer has to come from someone actually in your community |
| One answer per Discord account | Enforced by a unique index, not by a hopeful check |
| Straightlining detection | An answer that ticks the same box all the way down is flagged |
| Activity before the campaign started | Records that appeared the night before a campaign buy nothing |

Anything that fails lands in a **review queue** with the reason kept — eight distinct states, never
collapsed to a yes/no — and is worth nothing until a human approves it.

## What you can see as a community owner

Aggregates only — and nothing at all until **ten** people have answered, because below that a
breakdown identifies individuals in a small community.

<Callout type="warning">
  **Free-text answers are never shown to you**, at any number of responses. That is enforced on the
  server: the owner-facing route does not even compute them, so there is nothing to reach through the
  API either.
</Callout>

## Caps, and what they are for

- **50 %** maximum per community.
- **Next purchase only** — a rebate is not cash and is not paid out.
- **A wave budget** in euros. Waves run twice a year with a fixed window; when a wave's budget is
  used up, campaigns keep running and the rebate is booked into the following wave.

The caps, not the verification, are the real protection: the module can make abuse expensive, it
cannot make it impossible.

## Privacy

Participants consent before the first question and can withdraw afterwards. Ninety days after a wave
ends, the identifying fields — Discord id, identifier, IP hash and user-agent hash — are dropped; the
anonymised answers remain. The full wording is in the participation terms linked from the survey
itself and in the [privacy policy](https://lacore.netica.dev/legal/privacy/).

Both questionnaires are bilingual (**English and German**) and versioned: editing a published survey
creates a new version, so answers stay attached to the questions that were actually asked.

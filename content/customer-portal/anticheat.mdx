# Anticheat flags

**Dashboard → Anticheat** is where every detection your servers reported ends up. It is a review
surface, not a control panel: you read what happened, and you tell LACORE when it got something
wrong.

<Callout type="info">
  The anticheat ships **on**, in **observe mode** — it scores and logs what it *would* have done and
  punishes nobody until you switch it to enforce. This page is how you check its accuracy before you
  do that. See [Anti-Cheat & Security](/anticheat).
</Callout>

## What the page shows

At the top, three numbers:

| Tile | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Observed only** | The action was decided but not carried out — observe mode |
| **Called wrong** | Detections you marked as false positives |
| **Whitelisted hits** | Flagged, but the player or detection was exempt, so nothing happened |

Below that, **repeat offenders** (who is being flagged again and again), **detections you have called
wrong** (grouped, so a noisy detection is obvious) and the full flag list.

## The flag list

Each row carries **when**, **player**, **detection**, **action**, **trust score** and **server**, and
can be filtered by severity. Two pills explain themselves on hover:

- `observed` — observe mode: this action was decided, not carried out.
- `exempt` — whitelisted: logged, but never acted on.

Open a row and you get the detail: the trust score at the time and the player's identifiers.

## Marking a wrong call

Every row has a **wrong call** button. Marking a detection counts against how accurate that detection
is — that is the point of the number, so mark honestly. The same button undoes it and counts the flag
as real again.

<Callout type="warning">
  Flags reach the dashboard only while your server sends telemetry — that means a valid licence key in
  `server.cfg`. No key, no flags on this page. See [License keys](/customer-portal/license-keys).
</Callout>

# Support tickets

**Dashboard → Support** opens a private ticket with the team. Pick a category, describe the problem,
and the conversation continues in your own private Discord channel — you can reply from either side,
the dashboard or Discord.

## Opening a ticket

Categories: **Support · Bug report · Installation & setup · Purchase & license · Feature request ·
Report a player · Partnership · Other**. Write up to 1800 characters, hit **Open ticket**, and you
get a ticket number plus a link into Discord.

Your tickets are listed underneath with their state:

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **OPEN** | Still being worked on. |
| **CLOSED** | Closed by the team. |
| **RESOLVED** | Closed and you marked it as solved. |

Click one to read the whole conversation and reply — replies work while a ticket is open.

<Callout type="info">
  **Tickets are personal.** They belong to the Discord account that opened them, and teammates
  acting for your account never see them.
</Callout>

## Which channel for what

| Situation | Best route |
|---|---|
| A bug you hit while playing | [`/lacorereport` in-game](/user-guide/bug-reports) — fastest, carries your unit context |
| Install or license trouble | A ticket here |
| A question others may share | The [community repo or Discord](/support) |
| Feature idea | [Feature request](/support) so others can vote on it |

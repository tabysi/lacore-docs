# Activity log

**Dashboard → Activity log** is the audit trail of your LACORE account: who did what, and when.

It exists because of [Team](/customer-portal/team). The moment more than one person can change your
servers, "who turned that off?" becomes a real question — and this page answers it without anyone
having to remember.

## What is recorded

Account-level actions, each with the person who performed it:

- License keys created or revoked
- Servers renamed, linked or removed
- Configuration changed from the dashboard
- Teammates added, changed or removed

Filter by actor with the chips at the top — **Everyone** by default, or one person at a time.

## Configuration changes show the diff

A settings change is not just "config changed". The entry opens into a table of exactly what moved:

| Setting | Was | Now |
|---|---|---|

That is the difference between an audit log and a list of timestamps.

<Callout type="info">
  This log covers the **portal**. In-game staff actions are a different log — Big Brother, `/bblog`,
  see [Admin panel](/user-guide/admin-panel).
</Callout>

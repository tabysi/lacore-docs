# Bug reports

**Dashboard → Bug Reports** collects what your players send with `/lacorereport` in game.

<Callout type="info">
  These reports go to **the LACORE dashboard**, not into your own server database — they are about
  LACORE itself. A player never needs an account to file one.
</Callout>

## The list

| Column | What it is |
|---|---|
| **When** | Time the report was filed |
| **Player** | The account that reported |
| **Character** | Which character they were playing |
| **Category** | Picked by the player (configurable in `configs/cfg-report-sh.lua`) |
| **Server** | Which of your servers it came from |
| **Status** | Where the report stands |

Open a report and you see the account name, the callsign and the department the player was on duty
for — usually enough to reproduce the situation without asking them.

## Reporting more than once

Reports from the same player are grouped, so a single person filing five times does not look like
five separate problems. Repeat reports about the same thing are the useful signal here.

## Configuring the in-game side

The command name, the cooldown, the maximum length and the category list all live in
`configs/cfg-report-sh.lua`. The player-facing side is documented in
[Bug reports](/user-guide/bug-reports).

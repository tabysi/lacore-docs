# What's New

**Current version: 3.5.7.** One release, written as one page — what LACORE gained, grouped by what it
does for your server rather than by the day it was built.

## Law enforcement, end to end

Thirteen agency terminal skins on one shared backend, a redesigned **Sheriff CAD** built around real
dispatch procedure, a retro **9100-T** terminal, and the **Pennsylvania CAD** with a Call Detail view
that shows the whole incident instead of one clipped row. **Resolve** and **Detach** are separate
actions now, so going clear on yourself no longer closes the call for the entire shift.

Add to that the **ALPR** plate reader, **five report forms** (CHP-180, DUI, use of force and more)
with a format for writing your own, and **per-officer statistics** every officer can read themselves.

## Investigation you can actually run

Scene traces stay at an incident, an officer collects them, the lab turns a sample into a record, and
a supervisor approves the **warrant** that comes out of it. Evidence pins to a case, and the file
follows the person.

<Cards>
  <Cards.Card title="Investigation & evidence" href="/features/investigation" arrow />
</Cards>

## A server that explains itself

LACORE looks at your framework and your other resources **before** it complains, and writes a setup
report instead of a wall of errors. **Auto-Config** can apply what it suggests and revert it again,
region presets set up a whole city in one line, and `/lacoreconfig restore` now keeps the values you
configured instead of handing back empty defaults.

## The civilian side

The **Citizen Hub** gives civilians a panel of their own, a **gameplay layer** puts damage and combat
balance into configuration you can change live from the dashboard, and new players get a first-join
loop instead of a blank spawn.

## Your community, on the web

Your portal account now carries a **public community page** per server — departments, applications
and a live roster, without exposing anyone's Discord id. The **web dispatcher** is published, the
admin panel shows network activity, and support tickets keep the category you picked.

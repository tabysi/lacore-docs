## 3.4.x — Config & dependency housekeeping

A cleanup series that makes LACORE easier to own:

- **Three dependencies, honestly** (3.4.5) — the dependency story now matches the code. Hard
  requirements are exactly **oxmysql**, **pma-voice** and **spawnmanager**; `blip_info` and
  `bob74_ipl` became optional and auto-detected, and the long-dead `NativeUILua_Reloaded` is gone
  from every install doc.
- **config.lua on a diet** (3.4.4) — ~1000 lines of data tables (agencies, bus routes, game text,
  phone icons) moved out of `config.lua` into topic files. Backward compatible: an old edited
  `config.lua` keeps winning.
- **`configs/CONFIG-INDEX.md`** (3.4.2) — the 52-file configs folder now ships its own map, one
  line per file, grouped by topic.
- **The Discord role is the permission** (3.4.1) / **a supervisor group, not a list of names**
  (3.4.0) — the supervisor panel is now gated by an **ACE object** or directly by a **Discord
  role**, instead of hand-maintained license allowlists.

## 3.3.2 — Pennsylvania CAD, a replay studio & an anticheat you can review

The biggest release LACORE has had:

- **Pennsylvania CAD** — a sixth terminal in the style of a nineties county CAD. A re-skin, not a
  second dispatch system. Paid add-on, unlocked by license key.
- **Replay & Director's Cut** — record what happens around you, rebuild the scene and cut it with
  a real camera: four modes, keyframes, shot list, letterbox, grain, grades and depth of field.
  Made for trailers.
- **The in-car CAD screen became usable** — legible, per-seat terminals, `/cad` leans the camera
  in and hands you the mouse.
- **An anticheat you can review** — ships **on** for the first time, in a new **observe mode**:
  it detects, scores and logs what it *would* have done, but never punishes until you switch to
  enforce. Plus a dashboard review page with false-positive marking.
- **The menu library is gone** — settings, job picker, session menu, vehicle spawner and more are
  proper NUI windows now.
- **Agencies can be gated** per department by Discord role or ACE (`DutyAccess`), and txAdmin bans
  finally land in LACORE's ban list.

## 3.3.0 / 3.3.1 — Config editor, model library & grown-up dashboards

- **Config editor 2.0** (3.3.0) — dashboard settings are real controls now: sliders, colour
  swatches, keybinds, searchable model pickers and coordinate fields, validated on both sides.
- **Model library** — `/modelscan` inventories your server, `/modelcapture` photographs it,
  `/preview` shows any model in 3D in-game.
- **Rebuilt player list** — the list behind **I** became a real interface (a duty board grouped
  by department).
- **Dashboards grew up** (3.3.1) — admin Products / Player-database / Customer-database pages, a
  partner payout ledger with analytics, support-ticket overhaul, and the **paid add-on** system
  that unlocks features per license key.

## The LACORE Customer Portal

Manage everything about your LACORE license from one place at
**[lacore.netica.dev/dashboard](https://lacore.netica.dev/dashboard/)** — sign in with the Discord
account that owns your license, no separate password needed.

- **License keys** — generate a key, drop `setr lacore_license_key "…"` into your `server.cfg`, and your
  server links to your account automatically.
- **Servers** — see your linked servers with live status, players and version.
- **Server config online** — change LACORE's settings from the web, no file edits or re-upload; your
  server pulls the changes by key. Safe by design — only a vetted, typed set of settings, never files or
  code.
- **Payments** — your Tebex purchases in one list.
- **Partner program** — referral stats and payouts for partners.
- **Team** — give teammates scoped access to your account without sharing your login.

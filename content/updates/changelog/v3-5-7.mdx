# [3.5.7] – 2026-09-12 — Law enforcement, investigation and the customer portal

The 3.5 line, in one release. Six weeks of work on the law-enforcement stack, a new investigation
layer, the civilian Citizen Hub, a configuration system that explains itself, and a customer portal
that grew into a roleplay website. Everything below ships in **3.5.7**.

<Callout type="warning">
**Config change:** this release touches shipped defaults. `CallCenter.Debug` in
`configs/config.lua` is now `false` (the 911/311 call trace no longer floods your server console),
and `configs/cfg-server-sv.lua` ships empty Discord role ids. `/lacoreconfig restore` keeps the
values **you** configured — see *A restore keeps your settings* below — but check your role ids
after updating.
</Callout>

### Law enforcement terminals

![One shared CAD backend powering every LACORE terminal](/img/mdt/terminal-family.svg)

Every terminal reads the same dispatch data — a call raised on one is a call on all of them.

- **Thirteen agency skins.** Six more agencies got their own terminal look: CHP, state police,
  park rangers, marshals and more. One department tag picks the right one.
- **LASD MDC — full redesign** around real sheriff dispatch procedure, with its own incident
  backend, roster and status flow.
- **Pennsylvania CAD** — a retro county terminal, with a **Call Detail** view that shows the whole
  incident instead of one clipped table row: caller, reported text, notes, attached units and the
  complete log, with a comment field on the same screen.
- **Resolve and Detach are separate actions.** F11 is **Resolve Incident** and says, above the
  disposition codes, that it closes the call for every unit and every terminal. **SHIFT+F11** is
  **Detach**: it clears *your* unit and leaves the call open for everyone still on it. The footer
  status chip is a picker now.
- **ALPR** — the plate reader: automatic plate recognition with hotlist hits.
- **Five report forms** (CHP-180, DUI, use of force and more), plus a format for writing your own.
- **Officer statistics** — every officer can see their own numbers: calls, arrests, citations,
  time on duty.
- **The 9100-T retro terminal ships as its own product** (`lacore-90s-cad`), and the LEO half of
  LACORE ships as **LACORE MDT**.

### Investigation and evidence

![From a scene trace to a warrant](/img/changelog/investigation-flow.svg)

A first, working investigation chain: **scene traces** left at incidents, an **evidence collector**,
a **lab** (`/evidence analyze`) that turns a sample into a record, and **warrants**
(`/warrant request | list | approve | deny`) that attach to a person's file. Evidence can be pinned
to a case, and a supervisor decides what becomes a warrant.

### Civilian side and roleplay

![The Citizen Hub](/img/changelog/citizen-hub.svg)

- **Citizen Hub** — the civilian overhaul starts with a panel of its own (`/citizen`): services,
  documents and requests in one place.
- **A gameplay layer with real dials** — damage multipliers and combat balance are configuration,
  live-updatable from the dashboard, plus a first-join onboarding loop for new players.
- **Every player decides where their phone sits**, the settings screen stopped being a wall of text,
  and prompt dialogs take more than one line.

### Server setup and configuration

![The setup report](/img/changelog/setup-report-flow.svg)

- **LACORE looks around before it complains.** It detects your framework, your other resources and
  the overlaps between them, and writes a **setup report** instead of a wall of errors.
- **Auto-Config now applies what it suggests** — `/lacore autoconfig apply | revert`, with a clear
  precedence: your config files lose to an applied value, and both lose to the dashboard.
- **Region and street presets** — a whole city in one config line.
- **The config editor covers every setting**; two groups that had no tab and one key that had no
  field are fixed.
- **A restore keeps your settings.** The three-way merge behind `/lacoreconfig restore` used to
  resolve a conflict by taking the new default wholesale — which blanked the Discord role ids every
  owner had filled in. A conflicting region is now reconciled setting by setting: a value you
  changed stays yours, while new structure and new keys still come from the update.
- `/lacoreconfig undo` got the command it was missing, backups say **where** they live and whether
  they survive an update, and an unreadable database row is reported instead of being overwritten.
- **Features as inventory items** — anything LACORE ships can be bound to an item, and the
  permission system is configurable again.

### Customer portal and community

![What a survey answer has to pass to count](/img/changelog/survey-verification.svg)

- **A community page** turns the portal into a public roleplay website per server: departments,
  applications and a live player count, without exposing anyone's Discord id.
- **A survey module with a verified rebate** — two questionnaires, Discord-verified answers, a frozen
  denominator and an append-only reward ledger. Owners see aggregates only, never a single response.
- **Network activity** in the admin panel, **legal pages** and a public contact form.
- **The web dispatcher is published** — dispatch from a browser, gated by Discord role.
- **Support tickets keep the category you picked.** Four of the eight categories used to arrive as
  *Other*, losing the category's guidance and the priority a player report opens at.
- **A teammate with Config access can find the Config page** — the sidebar link asked for a
  different permission than the page itself.
- Two servers on one account show as **two rows**, as they should.

### Reliability and security

- **CAD data no longer goes to everyone on the server** — dispatch payloads are scoped to on-duty
  units, and reading the CAD needs a badge.
- **A wanted person can no longer clear their own warrant**, and police entries cannot be deleted
  from the file they were written on.
- **CAD access ends with the shift** it was granted for.
- **Callsigns are shaped before they reach the roster**, so two units sharing one no longer blank
  the unit list.
- **`AMR` — the in-game EMS job — is recognised everywhere**, including start and end of watch.
- **Dispatch radio arrives**: "Start of Watch" reached nobody, and `/xmit` could transmit an empty
  line.
- `/onduty` could die with `table expected, got nil` when a foreign resource sent a bad player list.
- **Dying no longer destroys the weapon you were holding**, and the half-built weapon-drop system
  that caused it is gone.
- **The alert webhook is a convar** (`set lacore_alert_webhook`), never a URL in the source.
- **LACORE starting before ESX** no longer breaks half the server, and QBox servers get their names
  and jobs through.
- Phone fixes: `/hangup` no longer leaves the phone stuck on the call screen, and dialling 911 or
  311 from the keypad works and says it once.

> **Not tested in-game.** Every Lua file passes the syntax check, the NUI, portal and docs builds are
> green, the config merge is covered by `tests/configmerge.test.lua` (20 checks), and the terminals
> were driven in the browser preview. The server-side paths in this release have not run on a live
> server.

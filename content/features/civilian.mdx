# Civilian Layer

The civilian layer gives non-LEO players meaningful roleplay tools — and, since 4.2.0, a way to
**answer what the police write about them**: pay a fine, see a warrant, release an impounded car,
report their own vehicle stolen, work a shift.

LACORE still has **no money of its own**. Where money is involved (fines, the impound fee, job pay)
it uses the **framework's** money when ESX, QBCore or QBox is running, and falls back to an RP
confirmation on a standalone server. Progression is handled by the achievements/XP layer.

![The civilian layer — radial, tools, orgs and opt-in turf](/img/features/civilian-layer.svg)

## Emotes (bridged)

LACORE does **not** ship its own animation engine — it would clash with the emote resource most
servers already run. Instead it **auto-detects and bridges** to whichever of these is installed:

| Resource | Detected name(s) |
| --- | --- |
| [DPEmotes](https://github.com/andristum/dpemotes) | `dpemotes` |
| [RPEmotes-Reborn](https://github.com/alberttheprince/rpemotes-reborn) | `rpemotes` / `rpemotes-reborn` |

The civilian **radial** plays the mapped emote through the detected resource. Configure the mapping
in `cfg-civilian-sh.lua` — each entry names the emote to play:

```lua
-- cfg-civilian-sh.lua
CivConfig.emotes = {
  { cat = "Gestures", id = "wave",    label = "Wave",     emote = "wave" },
  { cat = "Poses",    id = "handsup", label = "Hands Up", emote = "handsup" },
  -- cats: Gestures / Poses / Idle / Fun · emote = the DP/RP emote name (run /emotemenu in-game to see them all)
}
```

<Callout type="info">
  Run `/emotemenu` in-game to list the exact emote names your emote resource provides, then use those
  in the config. If neither emote resource is installed, the radial buttons notify the player.
</Callout>

Provider exports: `GetEmoteProvider`. Play routes through `/e <name>`; cancel is `/emotecancel`
(RPEmotes) or `/e c` (DPEmotes), handled automatically.

## Tools & identity

- **Digital ID** — a driver licence / ID card built from the active character (name, DOB, photo).
  Show it to nearby players (they receive it on their screen). The licence block is real: class,
  endorsements (boating, pilot, CCW, hunting) and a **SUSPENDED / REVOKED** stamp come from the
  character's licences. A character with no driver licence gets an *Identification Card*.
- **Self-service vehicle registration** — register a plate to your character; it then appears in LEO
  queries and on your profile.
- **Props** — place cones, barriers, signs, chairs, etc. Server-synced (everyone sees them), with a
  per-player limit; pick up / delete your own.
- **Character presets** — the `/char` form has a *Presets* section: save the filled form under a
  name, load it back later, delete it. Unlimited, per player, kept on the server.

## Citizen Hub

One panel for everything a civilian owns and owes, opened from the radial
(**Services → Citizen Hub**) or with `/citizen`:

![The Citizen Hub — Legal and Vehicles tabs](/img/features/citizen-hub.svg)

- **Overview** — the character as the ID card shows it: name, date of birth, gender, address, job,
  faction, phone, physical description, licences. A licence an officer suspended or revoked reads
  red, with who did it and why. *Show my ID* from here.
- **Legal** — an active warrant with its reason, a running jail sentence, and every citation and
  arrest with the **status of its fine** (unpaid, overdue, paid, waived) and a **Pay** button.
- **Vehicles** — every registration with insurance, a stolen flag and the impound lot. Insure or
  cancel, **report it stolen** (and withdraw that), **release it from impound**, remove the
  registration, or register another one.
- **Jobs** — each job with its pay, your shifts and stops so far, and a *Set waypoint* button to its
  depot.
- **Organisation** — your org, rank and member count; opens the organisation panel.

A red dot on a tab means something there wants attention. Anything that costs money or cannot be
undone takes **two clicks** — the button arms first. Every action is checked on the server; the
panel only asks. Escape closes it.

![What an officer writes, what the hub shows, what the civilian can do](/img/features/citizen-loop.svg)

### Fines

A citation or arrest with a fine is **owed** until it is paid. It starts *unpaid* with a due date
(`CivConfig.fines.dueDays`, default 7) and turns *overdue* after that — for the civilian in the hub
and for the officer on the person card, where the priors strip also shows the unpaid total.

What **Pay** does depends on `CivConfig.fines.payment`:

| Value | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| `"auto"` *(default)* | Framework running → the amount is taken from the bank account, cash if the bank is short. Standalone → an RP confirmation: the fine is marked paid. |
| `"rp"` | Always the RP confirmation, even with a framework. |
| `"external"` | LACORE only fires `lacore:api:finePayRequested`. Your economy script charges the player and calls `exports.lacore:MarkFinePaid(license, recordId)`. |

An officer can **waive** an unpaid fine from the person card. Nothing happens automatically when a
fine goes overdue — what that means is your roleplay.

<Callout type="info">
  Citations written **before 4.2.0** have no status and are never billed afterwards. Only new ones
  are owed.
</Callout>

### Licences

Officers can **suspend, revoke and reinstate** a licence from the person card (*Licence* button).
The hold belongs to the officer the way a warrant does: on a standalone server players set their
own licences in the profile, and a held licence can no longer be saved back to *Valid*. A framework
licence import does not overwrite it either. *Reinstate* puts the licence back to what it was
before the hold — it never grants a licence the person did not have.

### Vehicles

- **Stolen** — the owner of a registered vehicle can report it stolen; on-duty law enforcement are
  told, and ALPR and every plate query flag it. The owner can withdraw their own report. A flag set
  by the police (or by an officer's timed-out registration request) is cleared by an officer with
  *Clear stolen* on the person card — until 4.2.0 such a flag could never be removed.
- **Impound** — the owner is notified when their vehicle is impounded and sees reason, date and fee
  in the hub. With `Impound.ownerRelease = true` (default) they release it themselves; the fee is
  charged on a framework server.
- **Remove registration** — takes a `/vreg` registration off the character again. Vehicles that
  come from a framework garage cannot be removed here.

## Jobs

Real shifts instead of a waypoint chain: clock in at the depot, get a work vehicle, work the stops,
come back and clock out.

![A civilian job shift and the three shipped jobs](/img/features/civ-jobs.svg)

<Steps>

### Clock in

Stand on the depot marker (blip on the map, or *Set waypoint* in the hub) and press **E**. The
server hands out the stops of this shift and the work vehicle appears on the nearest road.

### Work the stops

Follow the GPS to each stop and do the action there — get out and empty the bins, hand over a
parcel, or wait at the bus stop with the bus at a standstill. Leaving mid-action interrupts it.

### Clock out

After the last stop, drive back to the depot and press **E**: the shift is counted and the
completion bonus is paid. `/quitjob` ends a shift anywhere, without the bonus.

</Steps>

| Job | Vehicle | Stops | At a stop |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sanitation Worker | Trashmaster | 6, fixed route | get out, empty the bins |
| Bus Driver | Bus | 8, fixed route | full stop, passengers board |
| Courier | Boxville | 5 of 10 stores, drawn anew each shift | get out, hand over the parcel |

The server checks every stop — the right one, in order, with the player standing there, and not
sooner after the last one than the work takes — so a shift cannot be reported from somewhere else.

**Pay** goes into the framework bank account on ESX / QBCore / QBox. A standalone server has no
money: the shift counts towards your totals and achievements. Clocking in also writes the job onto
the character — *Job* in the hub, *Occupation* on the officer's person card.

Add your own jobs, or change stops, pay and vehicles, in
[`CivConfig.jobs`](/configuration/civilian#civilian-jobs--civconfigjobs).

<Callout type="warning">
  The bus and sanitation stops are the kerbside bus stops LACORE already ships; the courier
  addresses are the standard store locations. The **depots** are a starting point — walk them once
  on your map and move anything that sits badly. The shift logic has not been run on a live server
  yet (see the 4.2.0 changelog).
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
  **Tow truck drivers** already had a real duty: `/onduty` with one of the tow companies puts you
  on the dispatch board, where *Tow Truck* requests arrive as calls.
</Callout>

## Organisations / gangs

An org framework lets civilians form groups:

<Steps>

### Create or join

Create an org with a name and a tag, or accept an invite from a member standing next to you. The
owner and officers set the message of the day. Membership and ranks are persisted server-side and
written to the character's `faction` field.

### Territory & visibility

With the opt-in turf system (below) orgs capture zones. The faction is **not** shown to law
enforcement in an MDT query — it is stripped from the result on purpose.

</Steps>

## Turf / gang war (opt-in)

An optional **kill-based territory capture** system. When a member of a rival org kills someone
inside a turf zone, it adds to that org's capture bar; fill the bar and the zone flips, awarding
**influence** (prestige — RP-only, no money).

<Callout type="warning">
  Turf is **off by default**. Enable it in `cfg-turf-sh.lua`:

  ```lua
  Turf.enabled = true
  Turf.zones = {
    { id = 'grove', label = 'Grove Street',
      center = vector3(-100.0, -1620.0, 30.0), radius = 90.0, color = '#2ecc71' },
  }
  ```

  Set your own zone coordinates — the samples are placeholders. Anti-farm protection requires the
  killer to be inside the zone and applies a per-victim cooldown.
</Callout>

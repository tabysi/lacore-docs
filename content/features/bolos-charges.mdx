# BOLOs & Charges

Two related LEO systems that write to a person's record and surface across every terminal: a
**BOLO** (Be On the Look-Out) alerts units to watch for a person or vehicle, and **charges /
citations** are driven by a configurable penal code.

## Where they show up

| Surface | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| MDT / CAD query | BOLO hits + charges/citations on the record |
| Dispatch console | BOLO list |
| Person card | Charges, citations, warrant status |
| [CCTV scanner](/features/cctv) | BOLO ACTIVE / ACTIVE WARRANT flags + recent records |

BOLOs are shared across the LAPD, LASD and Agency terminals — creating one anywhere is visible
everywhere, including the LASD CAD.

<Callout type="info">
  A person's **active warrant** also surfaces everywhere charges do, including a red **ACTIVE WARRANT**
  flag on the CCTV scan card. Any **on-duty unit** can set or clear a warrant straight from the person's
  record in every terminal (LAPD, LASD, Agency and the 9100-T) — the person can also set one on their
  own profile. Warrant changes are server-authoritative and written to the admin audit; supervisors see
  every active warrant at a glance in the [Supervisor Panel](/configuration/supervisor).
</Callout>

# Keybinds

The keys bound **by default**. Every player can rebind them in
**FiveM → Settings → Key Bindings → FiveM** (the engine remembers your choice).

| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| **O** | Open / close the MDT |
| **I** | Toggle the player list |
| **G** | Toggle vehicle engine |
| **K** | Seatbelt on / off *(only when `Features.seatbelt` is on)* |
| **U** | Hands up *(only when `Features.rpemotes` is on)* |
| **X** | Hand on holster *(only when `Features.rpemotes` is on)* |
| **X** | Put down carried person / uncuff nearest |
| **F1** | Open the phone |
| **F5 / F6 / F7** | Air Unit: heli cam / auto-orbit / spotlight |

<Callout type="warning">
  **X is bound twice** — "Hand on holster" and "Release carried / Uncuff nearest". Both handlers
  fire. Either turn `Features.rpemotes` off (if another resource owns your RP emotes) or rebind one
  of them in the FiveM key settings.
</Callout>

## While hands are up (`/hu` / **U**)
- **Crouch key** → kneel
- **Character-wheel key** → put hands on head

## Holster (`/hh` / **X**)
- Hold **aim** to draw your pistol from the holster
- Tap the character-wheel key to raise/lower the "hold there" hand
- Hold **sprint** to lower your hand without drawing

## Everything else

Many more actions ship **unbound** — the civilian radial, K9 orders, the in-car screen focus, the
fingerprint scanner, doors/trunk, firing mode and more. The complete list of all 32 bindings lives
in the [Command Reference](/commands) (Keybinds section at the top); assign keys to any of them in
*FiveM Settings ▸ Key Bindings ▸ FiveM*.

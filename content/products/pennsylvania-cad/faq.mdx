# Pennsylvania CAD — FAQ

## What do I have to install?

Nothing. The terminal ships **inside LACORE Core** and switches itself on once your licence key
carries the entitlement. There is no second resource to download or `ensure`.

## How do I point a department at it?

By name. Any department whose name contains `pennsylvania`, `penn cad` or `mobilecom` opens this
terminal instead of the LAPD one. Tagging a department before you own the add-on is harmless — the
tag is simply ignored.

## Is this a second dispatch system?

No, and that is the point. It is a **re-skin of the same backend**: the units and incidents on it are
the ones every other terminal sees. A call raised here appears everywhere, and a call resolved here
is resolved for everyone.

## What does it do that the other terminals don't?

Elapsed time instead of a clock, units on a call reading warm, per-user column switches, a
**Call Detail** view that shows the whole incident, **Create a Call** from the terminal itself, and a
hold-to-fire panic button.

## Does F11 close the call for everyone?

Yes — **F11 is Resolve Incident**, and the pane says so above the codes. If you only want to clear
*yourself*, use **SHIFT+F11 · Detach**, or the status picker in the footer. The full walkthrough is
in the [user guide](/user-guide/pennsylvania-cad).

## Can I change the call types and dispositions?

Yes, in `configs/cfg-penn-sh.lua` — call types, dispositions, the plate button label and the cosmetic
call-number prefix.

## Does it need LACORE Core?

Yes. It is an add-on to Core, not a standalone resource. For a standalone retro terminal, look at the
[9100-T CAD](/products/lacore-90s-cad).

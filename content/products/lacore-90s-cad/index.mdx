# 9100-T CAD — the 1990s in-car terminal

One terminal, nothing else. The **9100-T Mobile Data Terminal** is a nineties CRT computer bolted
into the dash: a photo-real chassis, keys you click, and the clack of a mechanical keyboard under
every keystroke. Behind the glass runs the full LACORE records backend.

![The 9100-T terminal](/img/mdt/terminal-family.svg)

It ships as its own resource — **`lacore-90s-cad`** — and needs neither LACORE Core nor a framework.

## What it does

- **Live calls.** Active incidents reach the terminal the way a unit gets them: panic buttons,
  backup requests, special-contact calls, traffic stops and on-views.
- **Unit status and self-assign** — take a call, update your status, close it with a disposition.
- **Person and vehicle lookups** against LACORE's records: licences, warrants, prior incidents.
- **Charges, citations, warrants and BOLOs** on a penal code you configure yourself.
- **EN · DE · RU**, like the rest of LACORE.

## What it deliberately is not

This is the honest part. The 9100-T product contains **one** terminal:

| Not included | Where it lives instead |
|---|---|
| LAPD, Sheriff, Agency and EMS terminals | [LACORE MDT](/products/lacore-mdt) or Core |
| The dispatch console | LACORE MDT or Core |
| Pennsylvania CAD | [Its own add-on](/products/pennsylvania-cad), inside Core |
| Phone, civilian layer, radio speech-to-text | LACORE Core |
| CCTV, K9, air unit, corrections, impound, fingerprint | LACORE MDT or Core |

The NUI is rebuilt from a scoped entry, so the other terminals are not merely hidden — they are not
in the shipped bundle at all. That keeps the download small and the interface honest.

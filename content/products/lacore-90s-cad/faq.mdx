# 9100-T CAD — FAQ

## Do I need LACORE Core?

No. It is a standalone resource with its own records backend. If you already run Core, you do not
need this product — Core ships the 9100-T as one of its terminals.

## Do I need a framework?

No. It runs standalone, and it also bridges to **ESX**, **QBCore** and **QBox** if you have one.

## Can my other departments get a different terminal?

Not in this product — it is one terminal by design. Multiple terminal skins are
[LACORE MDT](/products/lacore-mdt) or Core.

## Does it work with my existing police job?

Yes, through the framework bridge: on ESX/QBCore/QBox the job comes from your framework. Standalone,
duty and departments are LACORE's own, configured in `configs/cfg-agencies-sh.lua`.

## Where do calls come from?

From the game: panic buttons, backup requests, special contacts, traffic stops and on-views. There is
no separate dispatch console in this product — the terminal *is* the surface.

## Can I change the penal code, the labels and the language?

Yes. The penal code lives in `configs/cfg-charges-sh.lua`, terminal labels in the whitelabel config,
and the interface ships in English, German and Russian. Config files are escrow-open.

## Can I run it on two servers?

No. Every LACORE product is IP-locked to one server; a second server needs a second licence.

## How do I update without losing my configuration?

`/lacoreconfig backup` before, replace the folder, `/lacoreconfig restore` after. Settings you
changed stay yours.

# LACORE Products

Paid LACORE add-ons. Most are standalone resources you run **alongside LACORE Core** (or on their
own) — drop them in, configure them, done. Some, like the Pennsylvania CAD, already ship inside Core
and simply **unlock with your licence key**.

<Callout type="info">
  Looking for the **standalone MDX / CAD**? That's [LACORE MDT](/lacore-mdt). This page collects the
  *gameplay* add-ons.
</Callout>

<Cards>
  <Cards.Card title="Pennsylvania CAD" href="/products/pennsylvania-cad" arrow>
    A sixth terminal in the style of an old county CAD — unlocked by licence key, inside Core.
  </Cards.Card>
  <Cards.Card title="Farming" href="/products/farming" arrow>
    A complete farming job loop — harvest, process, sell.
  </Cards.Card>
  <Cards.Card title="Robbery" href="/products/robbery" arrow>
    Stores, ATMs and small-time robberies with dispatch integration.
  </Cards.Card>
  <Cards.Card title="Heists" href="/products/heists" arrow>
    Multi-stage crew heists with roles, loot and escape.
  </Cards.Card>
</Cards>

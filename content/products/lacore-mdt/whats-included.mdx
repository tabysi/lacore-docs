# What's in LACORE MDT

## Terminals and dispatch

- **Five Mobile Data Terminals** on one shared backend — the LAPD Mobile Client, the Sheriff's
  **PCMS**, the **Agency MDT** (thirteen agency skins: CHP, state police, park rangers, marshals and
  more), the **EMS/Fire CAD** and the **9100-T** retro terminal. The right one opens automatically
  for the player's department.
- **Live dispatch console** — incidents, units and callers on a real GTA tile map, with editable
  zones and districts, click-to-assign and CCTV markers.
- The [Pennsylvania CAD](/products/pennsylvania-cad) adds a sixth terminal as a paid add-on.

## Records

- **Person and vehicle files** — licences, priors, warrants, registered vehicles.
- **BOLOs** shared across every terminal.
- **Penal code, charges and citations** — offences with severity and fines, computed server-side.
- **Evidence and reports** — structured report forms (including CHP-180, DUI and use of force) and
  tagged evidence, visibility-gated on the server.
- **Investigation** — scene traces, lab analysis and the warrant flow that comes out of it.
- **Per-officer statistics** — every officer can read their own numbers.

## Field tools

- **CCTV** — fixed cameras plus placeable field cameras with a targeting scanner.
- **ALPR** — automatic plate reader with hotlist hits.
- **Corrections/jail**, **impound**, **K9**, **air unit** heli-cam, breathalyser and drug tests,
  mobile **fingerprint** scanner, and the New Address System.

## Under it

- **Big Brother action logging**, ACE permissions with an optional Discord-role bridge, and the
  hardened, server-authoritative security stack with its honeypot anticheat.
- **Gameplay balance layer** — damage and combat values as configuration.
- Config files ship **escrow-open** and commented; `/lacoreconfig backup` and `restore` protect your
  edits across updates.

## Not included

<Callout type="warning">
  These are part of [LACORE Core](/), not of LACORE MDT: the **NUI phone**, the **civilian layer**
  (emotes, props, organisations, turf), **radio speech-to-text**, the **web dispatcher** bridge and
  the **admin/staff panel** — plus the world and roleplay extras. Their config files are not shipped
  either.
</Callout>

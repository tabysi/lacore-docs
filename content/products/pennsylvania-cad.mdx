# Pennsylvania CAD

A **sixth terminal** for LACORE, in the style of an old county CAD: a left rail of F-key buttons, two
stacked tables — Current Units over Current Incidents — a command line along the bottom and a
`WAITING FOR RESPONSE` strip that lights up while the server is answering.

![Pennsylvania CAD screen layout](/img/mdt/penn-cad-layout.svg)

<Callout type="info">
  **Nothing to install.** Unlike the other products on this page, the Pennsylvania CAD ships **inside
  LACORE Core** and unlocks per **licence key**. Buy it, and the terminal turns itself on — no file to
  upload, no resource to start.
</Callout>

## What it is

A **re-skin, not a second dispatch system.** The units and incidents on it are the same ones the LAPD
Mobile Client, the dispatch console and every other terminal see. A call raised here shows up
everywhere, and a call cleared here is cleared for everyone.

What it does that the other terminals don't:

- **Elapsed time, not a clock** — how long a unit has held its status, how long a call has been open.
- **Units on a call read warm**, so who is committed and who is free is a glance.
- **Edit Columns** — each table's columns can be switched off individually, per user.
- **Call Detail** — double-click a call and read it in full: caller, reported text, notes, attached units and the whole incident log.
- **Create a Call** from the terminal itself, from a grid of call types you write.
- **A hold-to-fire panic button**, pinned to the bottom of the rail.

## Getting it

Departments opt in **by name**: any department containing `pennsylvania`, `penn cad` or `mobilecom`
gets this terminal instead of the LAPD one. Tagging a department before you own the add-on is
harmless — the tag is simply ignored and the department keeps whatever terminal it had.

Requires **LACORE Core** and an active licence key carrying the entitlement.
